What We Offer


From doorstep delivery to catering for get-togethers, Astro's African Foodies brings a rotating taste of South Africa to wherever you're eating.

🛵

Home Delivery

Order any day of the week and we'll bring that day's dish straight to your door, hot and ready to eat.

📅

Weekly Rotating Menu

A different traditional dish every day, from Mogodu Monday to 7even Colours Sunday, so there's always something new to try.

🍲

Bulk & Catering Orders

Feeding a family gathering, office lunch, or event? We cater larger orders of your favourite munchies on request.

📦

Pre-Order & Pickup

Prefer to collect it yourself? Pre-order through your account and skip the queue when you arrive.
