African Foodies


Welcome to Astro's African Foodies, a proud home of authentic South African flavour, born in 2026 out of a simple love for good food and good company.

We specialise in bringing the rich, vibrant taste of South African cuisine straight to your door, with convenient delivery that makes it easy to enjoy a home-cooked feel wherever you are.

What sets us apart is our love for variety: every single day of the week, we showcase a different cuisine from across the African continent, giving our customers a chance to travel through flavours without ever leaving their homes. From hearty traditional South African dishes to bold, spiced plates inspired by our neighbouring African nations, each meal is prepared with care, authenticity, and a deep respect for the cultures behind every recipe.

At Astro's African Foodies, we believe food is more than just a meal, it's a story, a memory, and a way of bringing people together. Whether you're craving something familiar or looking to discover a new favourite, we invite you to join us on this flavourful journey, one dish, one day, one delivery at a time.
